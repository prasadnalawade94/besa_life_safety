Specifications For Gas Detector

Commercial-grade LPG / CNG / PNG leak detection — Model No: BA GLD

Introduction

The Gas Detector Module is a reliable and efficient commercial-grade device designed to detect harmful or combustible gases in real-time. Engineered for safety and convenience, it features advanced detection technology, robust alarm systems, and seamless integration with external control devices. Its compact design, high durability, and adherence to regulatory standards make it suitable for various applications, including residential, commercial, and industrial environments.

Electrical Specifications

  • Input Supply: 220V AC
  • Circuit Supply: 12V DC

Technical Specification

  • Gas Sensor: LPG/CNG/PNG — capable of detecting target gases with specified sensitivity and accuracy.

Alarm System

  • Alarm Response time: ≤2 Sec
  • Warm-up Time: about 1 Min
  • Operating Temperature: -15°C ~ +55°C
  • Detection range: 3–5 MTR in radius
  • Storage Temp: -10 to 60 degrees
  • 12V DC Buzzer: Provides an audible alarm when gas detection reaches critical levels.
  • Alarm LED: Visual indicator that activates with the buzzer.

Relay

  • 12V DC Relay: Can control external devices in response to gas detection events.

LED Indicators

  • Power LED (GREEN): Indicates the unit is powered and operational.
  • Failure LED (RED): Activates in case of a sensor fault or system failure.
  • Alarm LED (YELLOW): Activates when gas sensor detects any gases.

Test Mode Switch

  • Allows the user to simulate (test) a gas detection scenario to verify alarm functionality and relay response.
Specifications For Gas Detector Document No:   Version: 1.0

Physical Specifications

  • PCB Dimensions: 65 × 66 × 20 mm
  • Enclosure: High-durability plastic material, flame-retardant, conforming to UL 94 V-0 flammability standard.
  • Box Dimensions: 112 × 80 × 39 mm
  • Device Colour: White

Environmental Specifications

  • Operating Temperature Range: 0°C to 50°C
  • Humidity Range: <90% RH non-condensing

Regulatory Compliance

  • Design to meet IS-11000 (Environmental and climatic testing)
  • Design to meet IS-16444 (Ensuring reliable performance and safety)
  • Design to meet RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances)
